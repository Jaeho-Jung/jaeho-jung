About

I graduated with a B.S. in Computer Engineering from Jeonbuk National University. My primary research interest is controllable symbolic music generation: how models can respond to harmonic and structural constraints without losing musicality.

I have implemented and compared RNN, LSTM, and Transformer architectures for jazz solo generation from scratch, and I am currently working on a Music Transformer variant that combines Jazz Transformer's representation with relative self-attention. I write about this process in an ongoing series, Jazz Is a Language.

I also care about turning models into usable systems. Through LetsPlay and smaller product experiments, I have worked across model exploration, API design, deployment tradeoffs, and mobile or web prototypes. I want this site to reflect both sides: research curiosity and the ability to build working AI products.

Selected AI Work

Music AI research and applied AI systems

These are the projects I would most want a professor or hiring team to see first. Jazz Is a Language shows my research direction and model-building practice; LetsPlay shows applied AI engineering, API work, deployment decisions, and team leadership.

Jazz Is a Language

2025 — ongoing

A symbolic jazz generation project on chord-conditioned improvisation: RNN, LSTM, and Transformer implementations built from scratch in NumPy, then ported to PyTorch for training on the Weimar Jazz Database. Current work extends this with a Music Transformer variant combining Jazz Transformer's Modified REMI tokenization (Wu & Yang, 2020) with relative self-attention. For hiring teams, this project also demonstrates sequence modeling, PyTorch implementation, model comparison, and technical writing.

LetsPlay

Applied AI, team lead

A speech-interaction system letting children role-play with an AI voice agent in real time. I led AI research and API development, including speech-model fine-tuning, API design, deployment tradeoffs, and project direction with a partner company. It is not my main music-generation project, but it shows how I approach applied AI systems outside the symbolic music domain: from research exploration to a working backend service for a real product context.
