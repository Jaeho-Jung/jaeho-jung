# Hi, I'm Jaeho Jung

I build AI systems that connect models, interaction, and creative tools. My
current focus is controllable music generation, especially symbolic jazz
generation and musical structure.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Engineering from Jeonbuk National University. My
work spans sequence modeling, speech interaction, API development, and applied
AI systems. I have also played guitar since 2014, and my experience with jazz
and improvisation shapes the questions I explore in music generation.

## Selected work

- [Jazz Is a Language](https://github.com/Jaeho-Jung/Jazz-Is-a-Language) -
  experiments in chord-conditioned symbolic jazz generation, from recurrent
  models to Transformers
- [MusicTransformer-Pytorch](https://github.com/Jaeho-Jung/MusicTransformer-Pytorch) -
  a Music Transformer implementation for controllable jazz solo generation
- [Let's Play AI Research](https://github.com/Jaeho-Jung/LetsPlay-ai-research) -
  speech and AI research for an interactive role-playing application for
  children
- [Let's Play Server](https://github.com/Jaeho-Jung/LetsPlay-server) - backend
  and API development for real-time voice interaction
